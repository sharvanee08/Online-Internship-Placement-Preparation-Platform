# Online Internship & Placement Preparation Portal

### Software Engineering Project | Group 15

A web-based platform for university placement cells that centralizes internship opportunities, applications, placement preparation, assessment, and student performance analysis.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Internship and placement activities are often managed through multiple disconnected channels for opportunity listings, applications, preparation resources, assessments, and performance tracking. This can make it difficult for students to manage their applications and preparation effectively, while placement officers have to coordinate student and internship information across different systems.

The **Online Internship & Placement Preparation Portal** aims to provide a centralized platform that brings these activities together while using assessment performance to guide students towards relevant preparation material.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The portal provides students with a single platform to manage their profiles, discover and apply for internships, practice placement-oriented assessments, and track their preparation.

Placement administrators can manage student information, internship listings, applications, questions, tests, and preparation material.

The system also analyzes assessment performance to identify areas of strength and improvement and uses these insights to recommend relevant preparation material.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Profile Management
Student and administrator accounts, academic details, skills, and optional resume management.

### 💼 Internship Management
Internship listings with eligibility information, search and filtering, and deadline management.

### 📋 Application Management
Online internship applications, eligibility validation, application status tracking, and deadline tracking.

### 📝 Placement Preparation
Domain-based questions, tests, practice, automatic evaluation, and test-result storage.

### 📊 Performance Analytics
Overall and topic-wise performance analysis, identification of strengths and weak areas, and progress tracking.

### 📚 Preparation Recommendations
Performance-based recommendations for placement preparation material relevant to identified weak areas.

---

## 🔄 How It Helps

The portal connects the internship and preparation workflow into a single cycle:

**Discover → Apply → Prepare → Assess → Analyze → Improve**

Instead of only providing test scores, the platform uses assessment results to help students understand their preparation gaps and focus on relevant practice material.

For placement officers, it provides a centralized system for managing internship opportunities, applications, student information, and preparation content.

---

## 🏗️ System Design Focus

The system is designed as a centralized web application with role-based access for students and administrators, supported by a shared backend and relational database.

Key design considerations include:

- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**
- **Modular feature design**
- **Relational database management**
- **Business-rule validation**, including internship eligibility
- **Persistent application and assessment history**
- **Secure authentication and password storage**
- **Responsive web interface**
- **Maintainability and reliability**

The architecture is intended to keep the major functional areas modular while allowing them to work together as a single platform.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend & Backend:** Next.js
- **Database:** MySQL
- **Authentication & Security:** Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), secure password hashing
- **Deployment:** Cloud deployment platforms such as Vercel or AWS
- **Communication:** HTTPS/TLS

The technology stack may be refined during development as implementation requirements evolve.

---

## 🎯 Expected Outcome

The project aims to simplify internship and placement management for universities while helping students make more informed decisions about their preparation through performance-based insights and recommendations.
